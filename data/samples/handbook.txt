Harbor Coffee Co. Team Handbook and Store Policies, revised September 2026.
This handbook covers the cafe, the roastery, the online shop, and wholesale accounts. Ask a manager when something is not covered here.
Section A: Hours and scheduling
The cafe is open 6:30 am to 6:00 pm Monday through Saturday and 7:30 am to 3:00 pm on Sunday.
Schedules are posted every Thursday for the following week, and shift swaps must be approved by a manager before the shift starts.
Baristas arrive 15 minutes before the doors open to dial in espresso and set up the pastry case; this setup time is paid.
Anyone working more than five hours gets a paid 20-minute break; shifts over eight hours get a second break.
If you are running late, call the store phone. A text message to a coworker does not count as notice.
Three no-call, no-show absences in a rolling 90-day period may lead to termination.
Section B: Pay, tips, and time off
Payday is every other Friday by direct deposit, and pay stubs are available in the staff portal.
Tips are pooled per shift and split by hours worked; managers do not take a share of the tip pool.
Overtime is paid at one and a half times the regular rate for hours beyond 40 in a week.
Full-time staff accrue one hour of paid time off for every 25 hours worked, up to 80 hours a year.
Paid time off requests need two weeks notice, and requests for holiday weeks are granted in the order received.
Everyone gets up to five paid sick days a year; a doctor's note is only needed after three consecutive days.
Jury duty is paid for up to five days when you provide the summons.
The cafe closes on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day; staff scheduled on other public holidays are paid double.
Section C: Leaving Harbor Coffee
Please give at least two weeks notice when you resign so we can cover your shifts.
Final paychecks are issued on the next regular payday and include any unused paid time off.
Return keys, aprons, and your name tag on your last day.
Section D: Appearance and conduct
Wear the Harbor Coffee apron, closed-toe non-slip shoes, and a name tag on every shift.
Hair longer than shoulder length must be tied back when you are behind the bar or in the roastery.
Phones stay in the back room during service; you may check them on breaks.
Headphones are not allowed while working the bar, the register, or the roaster.
Do not come to work under the influence of alcohol or drugs, and do not drink alcohol on the premises.
Smoking and vaping are allowed only outside, at least 25 feet from the entrance, and only on breaks.
Harassment of any kind is not tolerated; report concerns to any manager or email people@harborcoffee.example and we will respond within two business days.
We are an equal-opportunity employer, and hiring, pay, and promotion decisions are never based on protected characteristics.
Section E: Food safety
Wash your hands when you arrive, after breaks, after handling money, and after touching your face or phone.
Milk stays in the fridge at or below 40 F; a pitcher that has been out for more than two hours goes down the drain.
Steamed milk is never re-steamed, and pitchers are rinsed between drinks.
We label every pastry with its allergens; if you are not sure whether something contains nuts, say so and offer the ingredient card.
Oat, almond, and soy milk are available at no extra charge; ask about a milk allergy before recommending almond milk.
Anyone with a fever, vomiting, or diarrhea must stay home until symptom-free for 24 hours.
Section F: Coffee quality
Espresso is dialed in at open and after every hopper change: 18 grams in, 36 grams out, in 27 to 30 seconds.
Brewed coffee is dumped 45 minutes after it was brewed, whether or not the carafe is empty.
Retail bags are sold within 21 days of the roast date printed on the label; older bags go to the staff shelf.
Whole bean coffee stays in its sealed bag until it is ground; grind only what the customer needs.
Grinder burrs are brushed every night and deep cleaned every Sunday close.
The water filter is changed every three months, and the date goes on the sticker under the sink.
Section G: Customers, refunds, and complaints
If a customer is unhappy with a drink, remake it without asking questions and note it on the waste log.
A customer who says a bag of coffee tastes stale gets a fresh bag on the spot; take the old bag back so the roaster can check the lot.
Online orders that arrive damaged or wrong are refunded in full within 30 days of delivery; unopened equipment can be returned within 30 days.
Refunds over $100 need a manager's approval and are issued to the original payment method only.
Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash except where the law requires it.
Staff may not accept gifts worth more than $25 from vendors or customers.
Respond to online reviews only through the shared store account, and never argue with a reviewer in public.
Lost items go in the labeled bin under the register; unclaimed items are donated after 30 days.
Section H: Discounts and perks
Staff drinks are free during your shift and half price when you are off the clock.
Staff get 30 percent off retail bags and 20 percent off equipment; the discount is for personal use only.
Leftover pastries may be taken home at close after they are logged as waste; they may not be sold or served the next day.
Parking in the alley lot is free for staff on shift; use the two spots by the dumpster so customers can park up front.
Section I: Cash and security
Count your drawer at the start and end of every shift with a second person watching.
Drawers over $300 get a drop into the safe; only managers open the safe.
Never take a cash payment for a wholesale invoice; wholesale customers pay by card or bank transfer.
The alarm code is personal to you; do not share it, and tell a manager immediately if you think it has been exposed.
If a robbery happens, hand over the cash, do not resist, and call 911 once it is safe.
Cameras cover the register and the back door and footage is kept for 30 days.
Section J: Roastery and deliveries
Roasting happens Tuesday through Friday mornings; the cafe crew keeps the roastery door closed during production.
Green coffee sacks are stored off the floor on pallets, away from the wall, in the dry room.
Deliveries from vendors are checked against the packing slip before anyone signs; report shortages the same day.
Pallet shipments for wholesale accounts go out with Coastline Freight on Wednesdays.
Section K: Online shop and shipping
We ship to every US state and to Canada; US orders arrive in 3 to 5 days and Canadian orders in 6 to 10 business days.
Orders placed before noon ship the same day Monday through Friday; shipping is free on orders over $45.
Subscription customers can pause, skip, or cancel from their account page, and changes before the 25th apply to the next box.
We do not ship to PO boxes on orders that include equipment.
Section L: Wholesale accounts
Wholesale pricing starts at $14.50 a pound with a 10-pound minimum order.
New wholesale accounts pay on the first order; after that, terms are net 30.
Every wholesale account gets a free tasting visit and two hours of barista training per year.
Section M: Emergencies
The first aid kit is above the hand sink, and the fire extinguisher is by the back door.
If someone is choking or unresponsive, call 911 first and then use the AED mounted by the restroom.
When the power goes out, stop serving hot drinks after 30 minutes and move the milk to the walk-in cooler.
Section N: Training and growth
New baristas complete a two-week training plan with a trainer before working a shift alone.
We pay for barista certification courses after six months of employment, up to $500 a year.
Performance reviews happen every six months, and raises are decided at the same time.
Section O: Outside work
You may work elsewhere as long as it does not conflict with your scheduled shifts.
Do not share our recipes, roast profiles, or supplier prices with other cafes.
Section P: Pets and accessibility
Service animals are always welcome; pets are welcome on the patio but not inside the cafe, and staff may not bring pets to work.
The front entrance has a ramp, and the accessible restroom key is kept at the register.
Questions about this handbook go to your manager or to people@harborcoffee.example.

Check a damage photo before refunding

A customer sends a photo of a damaged delivery. One Jev call reads the picture: does it show the order, is damage visible, how bad is it, does it match what the customer wrote, and where is the damage? Code looks up the order, applies the refund window and dollar limit, and decides. Changing the policy re-decides without another call.

How the decision is made

Order status, delivery date, the 30-day window, and the refund amount come from the shop's records. Jev never sees the policy.

A Box question always returns four edges, even when nothing is there. Code shows the box only when the damage Noul says damage is visible, and pads it when the Box confidence is low.
